Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: July 23, 2026

At DocoDive, we respect your privacy and your right to control your personal data. This page explains how you can request the deletion of your account and all associated data.

Your Rights

Under applicable data protection laws, you have the right to request the deletion of your personal data. When you request deletion, we will remove all your personal information from our active databases, including:

  • Your profile information (name, email, username, avatar)
  • Your uploaded books and covers
  • Your comments, reviews, and ratings
  • Your download history, favorites, and reading streaks
  • Any social login associations (Google, GitHub, Facebook IDs)

How to Request Data Deletion

You can request deletion in two ways:

Email Request

Send an email to 7t7sufyan@gmail.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request". In your email, please include the email address associated with your DocoDive account so we can locate your data. We may ask for additional verification to confirm your identity.

What Happens Next?

  • Once we receive your request, we will acknowledge it within 48 hours.
  • We will process your deletion request within 30 calendar days.
  • After deletion, your account will be permanently removed and you will lose access to all services. Some anonymized data (e.g., aggregated statistics without personal identifiers) may be retained for analytical purposes.

Important Notes

  • Deletion is irreversible. Once your data is deleted, it cannot be recovered.
  • If you used social login, we also recommend revoking DocoDive's access from your Google/GitHub/Facebook account settings.
  • Books you uploaded that were approved will remain in the library for the public benefit, but your personal attribution will be removed.

Contact

For any questions about data deletion or our privacy practices, contact us at 7t7sufyan@gmail.com.
