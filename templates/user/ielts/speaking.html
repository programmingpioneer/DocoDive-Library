🏁 Final Exam Strategy

Before the test: prepare ideas, vocabulary and common themes. During Part 1: answer naturally and add relevant detail. During Part 2: use the one-minute preparation to create keywords and a simple story. During Part 3: give opinions, reasons, examples and consequences.

You do not need perfect English to achieve a strong IELTS Speaking score. Communicate clearly, develop your ideas, use appropriate vocabulary, and remain understandable throughout the conversation.

Smart Tips

💡 StructurePart 1: short + personal. Part 2: 2-min story. Part 3: opinion + reason + example.
⚠️ Biggest TrapMemorised answers sound unnatural and miss the actual question.
🎯 Band BoosterDevelop every answer with a reason and example — never one-liners.
⏱ Time SaverIn Part 2 preparation, write keywords, not sentences.
