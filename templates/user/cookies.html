    Optional tools start switched off

    Google Analytics and Brevo support chat are optional. You can accept or reject them separately. Your essential access to the library does not depend on accepting either category.

    1. Cookies and similar browser storage

    A cookie is a small item of information stored by your browser and sent with relevant requests. Local storage keeps information in the browser without automatically sending it with each request. Both can remember a choice or support a feature. This policy refers to these technologies together as browser storage where appropriate.

    Some storage belongs to DocoDive. Optional tools may also use their own cookies or identifiers when you enable them. The exact provider cookie names and lifetimes can change with the provider's configuration and browser behaviour.

    2. Essential functions

    Essential storage supports functions you request, such as remaining signed in, protecting forms and remembering your cookie choices. These functions are not switched off by rejecting optional tools.

    Storage or functionPurpose and duration
    DocoDive sessionSupports authentication, form security, verification steps and temporary service messages. Signed-in sessions are configured with a seven-day lifetime. Sign-out or an account security action may end a session earlier.
    Cookie preference recordThe local storage record docodive_cookie_preferences remembers your analytics and support choices in this browser for up to 180 days. It is not used as an advertising identifier.
    Requested appearance settingWhen you use the appearance button, docodive_theme saves your light or dark choice in this browser until you change it or clear browser storage. Before you make a choice, the site opens in Light mode. This preference does not enable analytics or support chat.

    A browser may clear data sooner, particularly in private browsing or when its storage settings require it. Blocking essential cookies can prevent sign-in, verification and some forms from working correctly.

    3. Optional analytics and support chat

    Analytics: Google Analytics

    If enabled, Google Analytics helps us understand visits, navigation and use of the website. It can process information about pages, interactions, browser or device characteristics and network requests, and may store cookies such as _ga or related identifiers. Its operation and retention depend on Google's service and our configuration.

    Analytics is off until you choose to allow it. Declining analytics does not prevent us from keeping the server records and operational activity counts needed to deliver, secure and operate the service, as described in the privacy policy.

    Support: Brevo Conversations

    If enabled, Brevo Conversations can load a support chat widget and use browser storage to maintain the conversation. Brevo can receive technical request details, conversation identifiers and any information you send through the chat, including a name or email address if you provide one.

    Support chat is off until you allow it. You can instead contact support@programmingpioneer.com without enabling the widget. Email delivery and account contact management through Brevo are separate server-side functions; the support preference controls the browser chat integration.

    4. Manage or withdraw your preferences

    Use cookie settings to allow or reject optional categories. Rejecting all optional tools is an available choice. You can reopen settings from this page or the site footer at any time.

    Your saved choice applies to the current browser and device for up to 180 days. It is not automatically synchronised with your account or another browser. When the choice expires, is cleared or is unavailable, optional tools remain off until you make a new choice.

    Withdrawing permission stops those optional integrations on subsequent page loads. The page may reload to apply a change to a tool already loaded. This does not undo information already sent to a provider or automatically erase every cookie set by that provider. Use your browser controls to remove remaining storage, or contact us about a separate data request.

    If JavaScript or browser storage is unavailable, the preference panel may not work or may not remember your choice. Optional tools remain off until the site has a valid choice allowing them. Essential account functions may also be affected by restrictive browser settings.

    5. Your browser controls

    Most browsers let you inspect, clear or block cookies and local storage through privacy or site settings. Search those settings for DocoDive to remove data for this site. You may also be able to block third-party cookies or clear data automatically when closing the browser.

    Clearing DocoDive's preference record resets your saved choice. Clearing session data can sign you out or interrupt verification. Browser privacy signals and blocking extensions can affect how third-party tools work; use the site's preference controls to make an explicit choice for the integrations described here.

    6. Other connections made by the site

    Cookie settings control the optional analytics and chat categories. The site also makes requests needed for its pages and features, including fonts, styles, scripts, document storage and a sign-in provider if you choose social sign-in. The receiving service may see your IP address, browser details and other normal request information.

    Rejecting optional tools does not disable these separate connections, server error diagnostics, security checks or account email. For a wider explanation of providers and information use, read the service providers section of the privacy policy.

    7. Updates and questions

    We may update this policy if our tools or storage practices change. The date above identifies the current version. New optional purposes may require a new choice before they are enabled.

    Send privacy or browser storage questions to privacy@programmingpioneer.com. If the settings control is not working, include the page URL, browser name and a brief description of what happened.
