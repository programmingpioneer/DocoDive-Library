    1. Treat people respectfully

    Discuss resources and ideas without attacking the people behind them. Different levels of experience, languages and perspectives belong in a learning community. Disagreement is welcome when it stays constructive and relevant.

    Do not threaten, harass, bully or organise abuse against another person. Do not post hateful attacks, degrading slurs or content targeting people because of personal characteristics. Unwanted sexual content, exploitation, intimidation and encouragement of violence are not acceptable.

    Avoid insults, repeated unwanted replies and attempts to provoke a conflict. If a discussion stops being productive, step away or report the problem privately. Do not encourage others to confront a user on another platform.

    2. Share material you are entitled to share

    Upload a document only if you own it, have permission to share it, or have another lawful basis to publish it through the library. Finding a free copy on another website does not establish that you can redistribute it. Keep copyright notices and credit the author or publisher accurately.

    Use a clear title, relevant category and description that reflects the actual file. Do not mislabel a file, use misleading covers, hide promotional links or repeatedly upload duplicate material. Describe limitations that would help another reader assess the resource, such as an older edition or an incomplete scan.

    Do not submit malware, deceptive downloads, illegal material, leaked confidential records or files intended to compromise another person's device. Do not use the library to distribute unauthorised personal data or bypass access restrictions.

    For a copyright concern, send the exact resource URL, a description of the protected work, your relationship to the rights holder and the reason you believe the use is unauthorised. The copyright reporting instructions explain the information needed and how to challenge a removal.

    3. Make reviews and comments useful

    Base a review on your experience with the resource. Explain what helped you, what did not and who may find it suitable. A specific observation about clarity, coverage, file quality or prerequisites is more useful than a personal attack or an unsupported accusation.

    • Keep comments relevant to the book, document or discussion.
    • Separate your opinion from a factual claim, and correct an error if you discover one.
    • Disclose a relevant connection to a resource you are reviewing, such as authorship or promotion.
    • Avoid repeated messages, unrelated advertising, affiliate spam and copied reviews.
    • Use a content warning or avoid unnecessary detail when discussing sensitive material.

    Do not pressure another person to change a review, arrange reciprocal ratings or post praise for a resource you have not examined. If a file is broken or a listing has the wrong information, a concise report with the URL helps us investigate.

    4. Protect privacy and safety

    Do not publish another person's address, private email, phone number, identity documents, account information or private correspondence without an appropriate basis. This applies to the content of a file as well as its description, cover, comments and screenshots.

    Protect your own information too. Never post passwords, verification codes, account recovery links or financial details. Use private email support for account and privacy issues; public feedback and comments can be read and copied by others.

    Do not impersonate a user, author, moderator or organisation. Do not claim an endorsement or official role that you do not have. Report suspected account compromise or exposed personal information through the contacts below.

    5. Keep participation fair

    Points, streaks, reactions and leaderboards are intended to reflect participation. Do not create duplicate accounts, coordinate artificial votes, automate activity or exploit a bug to manipulate these features.

    Respect the site's security controls and limits. Do not try to access private accounts or administrative tools, evade a restriction, disrupt downloads or overload the service. If you find a weakness, report it privately with enough information to understand the issue without collecting someone else's data.

    Do not use unsolicited messages, profile links or community features to run scams, solicit credentials or redirect people to unsafe services. An external link should be relevant and described honestly.

    6. Report a concern

    Send content, conduct or account security reports to support@programmingpioneer.com. For exposed personal information or another privacy concern, use privacy@programmingpioneer.com.

    Include the exact page or document URL, the relevant username if known, what happened and why you believe it needs review. A screenshot can help if it does not reveal unnecessary private information. Do not repost harmful material to draw attention to it or ask others to submit duplicate reports.

    Make reports honestly and provide context that could affect the decision. If you are the subject of a report or disagree with a decision, use the review route below. The contact page has detailed instructions for technical, copyright and accessibility reports.

    7. Moderation and review

    We consider the nature of the content, context, potential harm and a pattern of conduct when responding to concerns. Depending on the circumstances, we may remove or decline a contribution, restrict a feature, revoke sessions, warn a user or suspend or close an account. Serious safety, rights or security concerns may require immediate action.

    Moderation may involve mistakes or incomplete information. If you believe a decision is wrong, email support with the account concerned, the resource URL or decision details and an explanation supported by relevant evidence. Do not re-upload disputed material or create a new account to bypass a restriction.

    We cannot review every contribution before it appears or guarantee that every breach will be detected. These guidelines support the terms of service and may be updated as the community and service develop. Account deletion and public resource removal remain separate processes described in the data deletion guide.
